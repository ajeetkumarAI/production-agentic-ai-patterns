# KYC Requirements

Every new account requires a government photo ID and proof of address issued within the last 90 days.

Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is mandatory for customers whose expected monthly transaction volume exceeds
INR 10,00,000, for politically exposed persons, and for customers from high-risk jurisdictions.

KYC must be refreshed every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk and every
10 years for low-risk customers.
