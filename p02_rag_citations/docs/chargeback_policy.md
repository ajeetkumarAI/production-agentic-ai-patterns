# Chargeback Policy

Customers may raise a chargeback within 120 days of the transaction date for card-not-present purchases.
For card-present purchases the window is 60 days.

A provisional credit is issued to the customer within 10 business days once a chargeback is accepted for
investigation. If the merchant provides compelling evidence, the provisional credit is reversed.

Customers with more than three chargebacks in a rolling 12-month period are flagged for manual review and
may have card-not-present transactions restricted.
